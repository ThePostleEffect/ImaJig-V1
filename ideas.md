<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Neo-Brutalism / Pop Art
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **High Contrast & Boldness**: Unapologetic use of thick borders and saturated colors.
  2.  **Playful Geometry**: Emphasize the "puzzle" aspect with exaggerated shapes and hard edges.
  3.  **Tactile Feedback**: UI elements should feel "chunky" and clickable, mimicking physical puzzle pieces.
  4.  **Raw & Direct**: Minimal gradients, maximum clarity. Information is presented with stark confidence.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  -   **Intent**: Evoke the feeling of a toy box or a retro game. Fun, energetic, and stimulating.
  -   **Palette**:
      -   Background: Off-white or very light grey (e.g., `#F0F0F0`) to let pieces pop.
      -   Primary: Electric Blue (`#2D5BFF`) for main actions.
      -   Secondary: Vibrant Yellow (`#FFD600`) for highlights/accents.
      -   Borders/Text: Stark Black (`#000000`) for maximum definition.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  -   **Asymmetric Grid**: Use a bento-box style layout where containers have varying sizes and thick black dividers.
  -   **Floating Panels**: Controls and libraries float over the workspace with heavy drop shadows (hard, non-blurred).

  **Signature Elements**:
  -   **Thick Outlines**: 2px-4px solid black borders on buttons, cards, and the puzzle board.
  -   **Patterned Backgrounds**: Subtle halftone dots or grid lines in empty spaces.
  -   **"Jigsaw" Connectors**: UI containers might have decorative "tabs" and "blanks" to look like puzzle pieces.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  -   **Snap & Clunk**: Animations should be snappy (spring physics with high stiffness). Buttons depress visibly.
  -   **Drag-First**: The entire UI feels draggable and rearrangeable.

  **Animation**:
  -   **Hard Cuts**: Transitions are instant or very fast slides.
  -   **Wiggle**: Hover states cause elements to wiggle slightly, inviting interaction.

  **Typography System**:
  -   **Headings**: **Space Grotesk** or **Chivo Mono** (Bold/Black) – technical but quirky.
  -   **Body**: **Inter** or **DM Sans** (Medium/Bold) – highly legible, keeping the "raw" feel.
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Glassmorphism / Ethereal Zen
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **Calm & Focus**: The interface should recede, allowing the user to enter a "flow state" while solving.
  2.  **Translucency & Depth**: Use frosted glass effects to layer UI over the puzzle board without obscuring it completely.
  3.  **Soft Light**: Lighting should feel natural and diffuse, like sunlight on a table.
  4.  **Organic Shapes**: Rounded corners and fluid transitions to reduce cognitive load.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  -   **Intent**: Relaxation and mindfulness. A digital sanctuary for puzzle solving.
  -   **Palette**:
      -   Background: Deep, muted teal or slate (`#1A202C`) to reduce eye strain.
      -   Glass: White with low opacity and background blur.
      -   Accents: Soft Coral (`#FF8A80`) or Mint (`#A7FFEB`) for subtle indicators.
      -   Text: Off-white (`#F7FAFC`) for readability against dark backgrounds.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  -   **Central Stage**: The puzzle board is the absolute hero. UI elements are pushed to the edges and fade out when not in use.
  -   **Radial Menus**: Contextual tools appear near the cursor/finger to minimize travel.

  **Signature Elements**:
  -   **Frosted Glass**: Panels use `backdrop-filter: blur()` and white borders with low opacity.
  -   **Glows**: Active pieces or correct snaps emit a soft, breathing glow.
  -   **Floating Islands**: UI groups (timer, tools) are isolated islands rather than full bars.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  -   **Fluidity**: Movements are smooth and damped. Pieces "glide" into place.
  -   **Gentle Feedback**: Snapping is accompanied by a soft visual ripple and a subtle sound (if audio were added).

  **Animation**:
  -   **Fade & Float**: Elements drift into place.
  -   **Slow Easing**: Transitions use ease-in-out curves for a natural feel.

  **Typography System**:
  -   **Headings**: **Outfit** or **Manrope** (Light/Regular) – clean, modern, and friendly.
  -   **Body**: **Quicksand** or **Nunito** – rounded terminals to match the organic vibe.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Cyber-Industrial / HUD
  **Core Principles**:
  1.  **Precision & Data**: The app feels like a high-tech tool for reconstructing images.
  2.  **Monospaced Aesthetics**: Emphasize the "digital" nature of the puzzle (pixels, glitches, code blocks).
  3.  **High Functionality**: Dense information density, toggleable overlays, and technical visualizers.
  4.  **Dark Mode Native**: Built primarily for low-light environments.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  -   **Intent**: Focus and technical mastery. Like working in a darkroom or a command center.
  -   **Palette**:
      -   Background: Pure Black (`#050505`) or very dark grey.
      -   Primary: Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`) or Matrix Green (`#00FF41`).
      -   Secondary: Warning Orange (`#FF5F00`) for unsnapped edges.
      -   UI Lines: Dim Grey (`#333333`) for structural grids.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  -   **Dashboard**: Fixed sidebars with technical readouts (progress %, piece count, time).
  -   **Modular Grid**: UI components snap to a strict pixel grid.

  **Signature Elements**:
  -   **Scanlines**: Subtle CRT effects or scanlines on the background.
  -   **Corner Brackets**: UI panels are framed by technical corner markers `[ ]`.
  -   **Data Viz**: Progress bars are segmented; timers look like digital clocks.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  -   **Instant Response**: Zero latency feel.
  -   **Mechanical Snapping**: Pieces snap with a visual "lock" animation, like a bolt sliding home.

  **Animation**:
  -   **Glitch**: Occasional glitch effects on transitions (fitting the "Glitch Strips" cut style).
  -   **Typewriter**: Text reveals character by character.

  **Typography System**:
  -   **Headings**: **JetBrains Mono** or **Fira Code** – purely monospaced.
  -   **Body**: **Roboto Mono** or **Space Mono** – legible but technical.
</idea>
